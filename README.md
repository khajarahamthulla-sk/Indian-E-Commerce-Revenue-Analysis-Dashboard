## E-Commerce Revenue Analysis Dashboard

**1. Headline:** 📊 The E-Commerce Revenue Analysis Dashboard is a Dynamic interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built to analyze and visualize revenue performance across multiple business dimensions. 
___

**2. Purpose**
The E-Commerce Revenue Analysis Dashboard enables users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify revenue trends, evaluate regional performance, and make data-driven business decisions. This project demonstrates advanced Excel dashboarding techniques using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Conditional Formatting, and Maps.

**4. Data Source: Kaggle- [# Indian E-commerce Pricing, Revenue Growth]** The dataset is built to support pricing, revenue, and growth analysis, with a strong focus on understanding how discount strategies, competition, and customer behavior influence demand and revenue outcomes.


---
**5. Features / Highlights**
E-commerce companies in India face challenges in understanding which states and product categories drive the most revenue and units sold. The goal of the dashboard is to provide an analytical view of Indian e-commerce revenue performance, enabling decision-makers to: Revenue KPI, Units Sold KPI, Total Orders KPI, YoY Growth, Monthly Revenue Trend, Revenue vs Units Sold Analysis, Top 5 Product Categories, Revenue by Indian States, Gender-wise Revenue Share, Sales Event Analysis, Interactive Filters, Region, Year, & Month.

---
**6. Business Impact & Insights:**

-   **Overview:** Total revenue stands at ₹2,17,45,10,367 with 9,70,188 units sold across 30,600 orders, showing strong baseline demand.
    
-   **Categories:** Sports & Fitness, Footwear, Fashion, Electronics, and Beauty & Personal Care lead in sales volume, guiding inventory focus.
    
-   **Regional Performance:** State maps and regional filters highlight key geographic strongholds for targeted expansion.
    
-   **Events & Competition:** Analysis of Festival vs. Normal periods across competition tiers optimizes promotional and discounting strategies.
    
-   **Demographics:** Gender revenue share is evenly split (~50% Female vs. ~50% Male), indicating broad market appeal.
    
-   **Seasonality:** Monthly trend analysis maps out periodic fluctuations, aiding supply chain and stock planning.
